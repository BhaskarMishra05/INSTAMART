BUSINESS PROBLEMS - GROCERY E-COMMERCE DATASET
================================================
Dataset Context: Online grocery platform with orders, products, aisles, departments, and user behavior data.

================================================================
BUSINESS PROBLEM 1: Customer Churn & Retention
================================================================
PROBLEM:
The marketing team wants to know which customers are at risk of churning (stopping purchases).
"days_since_prior_order" is getting longer for some users — identify them before they leave.

BUSINESS QUESTION:
Which users have increasing gaps between orders, and what product categories are they abandoning?

ANALYSIS TASKS:
- Calculate average days_since_prior_order per user over time
- Flag users whose order frequency is declining
- Identify which departments/aisles those users shop in less
- Segment users: Active, At-Risk, Churned

BUSINESS VALUE:
Enables targeted retention campaigns (e.g., discount coupons sent to at-risk users).
Estimated impact: Even retaining 5% of at-risk users can significantly boost monthly revenue.

================================================================
BUSINESS PROBLEM 2: Product Reorder Optimization (Inventory & Recommendations)
================================================================
PROBLEM:
The supply chain team needs to know which products are frequently reordered so they can
ensure stock availability. The product team wants a "reorder reminder" feature.

BUSINESS QUESTION:
Which products have the highest reorder rates, and at what intervals do customers reorder them?

ANALYSIS TASKS:
- Calculate reorder rate per product (reordered=1 / total orders)
- Combine with days_since_prior_order to estimate reorder intervals
- Identify top reordered products by department and aisle
- Find products with high reorder rate but frequent stockouts (out-of-scope but flagged)

BUSINESS VALUE:
- Inventory: Prioritize stocking high-reorder products
- Product feature: "It's time to reorder your Milk!" push notification
- Revenue: Increasing reorder convenience directly increases order frequency

================================================================
BUSINESS PROBLEM 3: Market Basket Analysis (Cross-Sell & Upsell)
================================================================
PROBLEM:
The product and marketing team wants to increase average order value by recommending
products that are frequently bought together.

BUSINESS QUESTION:
What products are most commonly added to the same order, and can we identify strong
product pairings across departments?

ANALYSIS TASKS:
- Use order_products data to find co-occurrence of product pairs per order
- Calculate support, confidence, and lift for product associations
- Identify cross-department pairings (e.g., specialty cheese + prepared soups)
- Rank top 10 product bundle recommendations

BUSINESS VALUE:
- "Customers also bought..." recommendation engine
- Bundle promotions to increase basket size
- Targeted upsell ads based on what's already in the cart

================================================================
BUSINESS PROBLEM 4: Peak Demand Forecasting (Operations)
================================================================
PROBLEM:
The operations team needs to staff appropriately and ensure delivery capacity during
peak hours and days. Over- or under-staffing costs money.

BUSINESS QUESTION:
When (day of week and hour of day) do most orders occur, and how does this vary by
user segment or product category?

ANALYSIS TASKS:
- Aggregate orders by order_dow (day of week) and order_hour_of_day
- Create heatmap of order volume: day vs. hour
- Segment by new vs. returning customers (order_number = 1 vs. higher)
- Identify if certain departments (e.g., frozen food) peak at different times

BUSINESS VALUE:
- Staffing: Schedule more pickers/delivery drivers during peak windows
- Marketing: Run flash sales during off-peak hours to balance load
- Infrastructure: Auto-scale servers during predicted high-traffic periods

================================================================
BUSINESS PROBLEM 5: New User Behavior & Onboarding Success
================================================================
PROBLEM:
The growth team wants to understand what new users (order_number = 1) buy in their
first order and whether first-order behavior predicts long-term retention.

BUSINESS QUESTION:
Do users who buy from certain departments in their first order have higher lifetime
order counts? What does a "successful" first order look like?

ANALYSIS TASKS:
- Isolate first orders (order_number = 1) per user
- Map products to departments and aisles
- Compare department mix of first order vs. users who made 5+ orders
- Identify the "golden path" — what top retained users bought first

BUSINESS VALUE:
- Onboarding: Curate first-order product suggestions to increase retention
- Acquisition: Target ads toward users likely to buy high-retention product categories
- UX: Design new user homepage around departments that drive long-term engagement
