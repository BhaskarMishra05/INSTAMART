# Business Questions

## 1. Product & Category Performance

* Which products are ordered most frequently?
* Which departments generate the highest order volume?
* Which aisles generate the highest order volume?
* What is the reorder rate by product, aisle, and department?
* Which departments have:

  * High order volume but low reorder rate?
  * Low order volume but high reorder rate?

---

## 2. Customer Purchase Behavior

* What is the distribution of orders per customer?
* What is the average `days_since_prior_order`?
* What is the average basket size (items per order)?

---

## 3. Temporal Analysis

* Which day of the week has the highest order volume?
* Which hour of the day has the highest order volume?
* How do order patterns vary by day and hour?

---

## 4. Reorder Behavior

* What percentage of ordered items are reorders?
* Which products have the highest reorder rates?
* Does `add_to_cart_order` influence reorder likelihood?

---

## 5. Market Basket Analysis

* Which product pairs are most frequently purchased together?
* Which aisles or departments commonly co-occur in the same order?

---

## 6. Department & Aisle Segmentation

* Rank departments by total items sold.
* Rank aisles by total items sold.
* Identify the top 5 products in each department.
